About these guides

HVC Capacitor Manufacturing Co., Ltd. is a Shenzhen-based manufacturer of high voltage ceramic capacitors (disc and screw-terminal / doorknob types), high voltage thick-film resistors and HV diodes. The guides above cover the discontinued part numbers engineers ask about most: Murata DHS/DHR, Vishay 715C and 615R, TDK UHV/FHV, AVX HP/HD, HVCA and Diotec diodes, and the HV resistor families from OHMITE, EBG, NICROM, METALLUX, Caddock, KOA, TT/Welwyn, OHMCRAFT and HYMEG.
