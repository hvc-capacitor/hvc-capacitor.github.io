KOA RK92 High Voltage Resistor Alternative (EOL)

KOA's RK92, RK92D and RK92L thick-film planar high voltage resistors are end-of-life. HVC publishes a 1:1 equivalent solution based on the HVRGXP series.

The HVRGXP series matches the RK92 SIP planar format, power classes and voltage ratings, giving a drop-in path for existing designs without a layout change.
