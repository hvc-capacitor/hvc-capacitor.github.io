METALLUX HVR High Voltage Resistor Alternative

HVC's HVR series is pin-compatible with METALLUX HVR high voltage thick-film resistors, including the 967 / 968 / 969 families.

METALLUX HVR resistors and HVC HVR parts share the same mounting pitch and body styles; HVC publishes a technical note with the model-by-model mapping.
