SAMWHA High Voltage Ceramic Capacitor Alternative

HVC publishes replacement guidance for SAMWHA high voltage ceramic capacitors (disc and screw-terminal types) used in power supplies, X-ray equipment and industrial HV systems.

SAMWHA HV ceramic capacitors share the standard disc formats and ceramic dielectric classes that HVC manufactures in volume. Send a SAMWHA part number and HVC will confirm the matching rating, size and dielectric.
