SEMIKRON / SEMTECH High Voltage Diode Alternative

HVC's HVD series covers pin-to-pin replacements for SEMIKRON and SEMTECH high voltage diodes used in industrial rectifier and multiplier stacks.

SEMIKRON / SEMTECH avalanche and fast-recovery HV diodes are increasingly allocated; HVC manufactures the equivalent parts directly, including high-current types. Full cross-reference tables are published on the official site.
