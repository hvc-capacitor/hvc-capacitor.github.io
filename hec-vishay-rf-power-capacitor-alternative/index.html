HEC / Vishay RF Power Capacitor Alternative (HT50, HT57)

HVC's HVCHF series is built as a drop-in alternative for HEC HT50 / HT57 and Vishay Draloric RF power ceramic capacitors, for high-energy RF and semiconductor applications.

RF power capacitors work under high current and high dV/dt; HVC's RF line uses the same plate construction and ceramic dielectric classes as the parts it replaces. See the official RF capacitor page for ratings and sample options.
