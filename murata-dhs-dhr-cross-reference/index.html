Murata DHS / DHR High Voltage Capacitor Cross-Reference

Murata's screw-terminal (DHS) and radial-lead (DHR) high voltage ceramic capacitor series are discontinued. This page lists the discontinued Murata part numbers covered by HVC's published cross-reference tables, together with the drop-in HVC replacement, voltage, capacitance and dielectric data.

All replacements use the same N4700 low-loss dielectric as the original Murata parts and keep the industry-standard mounting dimensions, so no PCB or chassis change is required. Typical factory lead time for HVC high voltage capacitors is a few weeks, versus long allocation queues on the secondary market.
